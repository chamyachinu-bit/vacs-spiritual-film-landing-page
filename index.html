About Us

The Vaishvik Adhyatmik Chitranagari Samiti has been formed with the sole purpose to provide guidance, assistance and support to the Film and Entertainment industry. Through structured programs and personalized guidance, we aim to help members of the fraternity maintain their physical, mental, emotional, and spiritual well-being; especially in the challenging realities of today’s world.

Our objective is to nurture creative minds so they can remain true to their art and passion, rise above pressures and challenges of the field, and stand strong in the face of challenges. We envision transforming the industry by reconnecting it with its original roots, values, and cultural essence.

Our work is dedicated to spiritually empowering and strengthening the industry and its fraternity to become a positive, responsible, and inspirational force in society. We aim to nurture overall well-being while instilling right values and reinstating culture as the foundation for individual and collective growth.

We seek to help lead the industry toward its highest potential as a noble craft, with its roots restored in art, culture, and inner wisdom. As the industry achieves excellence in all dimensions, we ensure that each individual remains connected to and guided by their Divine Inner Voice.

By strengthening the spiritual core, empowering the mind, and refining action, we strive to make filmmaking and entertainment a deeply satisfying, fulfilling, and creative artistic expression—one that uplifts both the creator and society at large.

Our visionary and guiding light

Prem Avataar
Maitreya Dadashreeji

A Transformation Pioneer and Global Humanitarian

Maitreya Dadashreeji is our visionary and inspiration. Rendering selfless, social and spiritual service to mankind, He has successfully integrated ancient spiritual wisdom into modern concepts that offer practical applications for holistic growth in one's daily life.

By sharing His profound yet simple wisdom with incredible clarity and offering practical applications for holistic growth through true experience, He has redefined spirituality, leading many individuals from various religions, beliefs and nations on how to live meaningful lives, with positivity, compassion, love and unity. Under His guidance, thousands globally have experienced and witnessed an inner shift, re-establishing the true connection within and freeing themselves from false bondages, thus bringing about the much-needed transformation in them.

Awakening love within hearts, bonding people, removing boundaries, uniting nations, He is bringing about The Great Transformation by guiding humanity to walk on the path of Love and Selfless Service. He spearheads various national and international initiatives to uplift communities, leaving no section of society unturned. VACS is a step towards fortifying His larger vision of One World One Family One Truth.

“We have forgotten the Creator Himself and are lost in His creation, searching for peace all over this world, whereas peace lies in the true bonding with the Source of it all.”
