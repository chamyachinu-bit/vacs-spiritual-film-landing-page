Welcome to VACS

Guidance, assistance and support for the industry.

The Vaishvik Adhyatmik Chitranagari Samiti has been formed with the sole purpose of supporting the Film and Entertainment industry through structured programmes and personalised guidance.

We help members maintain their physical, mental, emotional and spiritual wellbeing—especially amid the challenging realities of today’s world. Our objective is to nurture creative minds so they remain true to their art, rise above pressure and reconnect with values, culture and their Divine Inner Voice.

A Transformation Pioneer and Global Humanitarian

Maitreya Dadashreeji is the visionary and inspiration of VACS. Rendering selfless social and spiritual service to mankind, He has successfully integrated ancient spiritual wisdom into modern concepts with practical applications for holistic growth.

Through profound yet simple wisdom, He has guided thousands across religions, beliefs and nations towards meaningful lives filled with positivity, compassion, love and unity.
