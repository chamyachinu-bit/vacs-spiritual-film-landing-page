Welcome

Guidance, Assistance and Support for the Industry

The Vaishvik Adhyatmik Chitranagari Samiti has been formed with the sole purpose to provide guidance, assistance and support to the Film and Entertainment industry. Through structured programs and personalized guidance, we aim to help members of the fraternity maintain their mental, emotional, and spiritual well-being — especially in the challenging realities of today’s world.

Our objective is to nurture creative minds so they can remain true to their art and passion, rise above industry pressures, and stand strong in the face of challenges. We envision transforming the industry by reconnecting it with its original roots, values, and cultural essence.

Our Visionary and Guiding Light

Premavatar Maitreya Dadashreeji

A Transformation Pioneer and Global Humanitarian

Maitreya Dadashreeji is the Founder-Visionary of Vaishvik Adhyatmik Chitranagari Samiti. Rendering selfless, social and spiritual service to mankind, He has successfully integrated ancient spiritual wisdom into modern concepts that offer practical applications for holistic growth in one’s daily life.

Awakening love within hearts, bonding people, removing boundaries, uniting nations, He is bringing about The Great Transformation by guiding humanity to walk on the path of Love and Selfless Service to establish His vision of ‘One World, One Family, One Truth’.

“We have forgotten the Creator Himself and are lost in His creation, searching for peace all over this world, whereas peace lies in the true bonding with the Source of it all.”

— Maitreya Dadashreeji

Vision

To transform the Film and Entertainment industry by re-establishing the art to its original essence and re-connecting the fraternity to their core — instilling the right values and nurturing overall wellbeing.

Mission
